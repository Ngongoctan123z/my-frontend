# TOPIK and HSK Writing bank

The bank contains 50 TOPIK II PBT sets (51–54), 50 HSK 5 sets and 50 HSK 6 sets. They are original practice exercises, not official released past papers. Admin preview: `/quan-ly-he-thong-123?tab=topik-hsk`; the same bank is also available from Writing exam management.

TOPIK follows the NIIED PBT guide: 51/52 have two sentence blanks each, 10 marks each; 53 uses a chart and 200–300 characters, 30 marks; 54 uses a social topic and 600–700 characters, 50 marks. See the official format guide linked in each set. These are not IBT tasks.

The 50 chart panels reproduce published figures from Korea's Ministry of Data and Statistics, *2025 한국의 사회지표*, published 31 March 2026. Source announcement: https://mods.go.kr/board.es?act=view&bid=10820&list_no=443371&mid=a10301060500 . Source PDF: https://mods.go.kr/boardDownload.es?bid=10820&list_no=443371&seq=12 . The PDF's cover identifies KOGL Type 3 (attribution, no modification). Each figure retains its caption, units and embedded source note; it is not redrawn or generated. Metadata records PDF page, attribution, file SHA-256 and manually checked numbers used for grading. Exercise directions select a manageable subset of the published figure and prohibit invented causes.

HSK uses the six-level (2.0) format, explicitly labelled in the UI; it does not claim to implement a different HSK 3.0 examination. HSK 5 contains 8 word-order questions 91–98 (40 marks), a five-keyword composition 99 (30), and a real photograph composition 100 (30), approximately 80 characters each. The 400 ordering sentences and 50 keyword exercises are original. Photos use the reviewed Unsplash library and include source/license links. No AI images are used. HSK 6 contains 50 original narratives, each 900–1200 non-whitespace characters, with 10 minutes reading and 35 minutes writing a roughly 400-character summary, free title and no personal opinion. Sources: https://admin.chinesetest.cn/godownload.do and its official HSK 5/6 sample papers.

`npm run prepare:writing-bank` validates the catalog and chart files, then writes `public/exam-data/writing-bank-v1.json`. The data factory runs at build time and is not imported by the Worker. The API loads the static asset through ASSETS, then atomically inserts 300 prompts and 150 linked parent records into D1. Insert chunks use at most 76 bound parameters. The marker `TOPIK_HSK_150_SETS_V1` prevents edited or deleted rows from being overwritten or resurrected. Broken child IDs exclude a set rather than silently choosing another prompt. Practice selection compares full prompt text; it does not equate HSK narratives merely because they share directions.

The new catalog and exact-set requests are public read operations; admin previews remain authenticated. Loading a question consumes no provider call or grading quota. Full TOPIK practice stores the exact prompt snapshots with history and keeps full-test and single-question drafts separate.
