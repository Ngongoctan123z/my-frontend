# TOEIC Writing emails and native task marks

60 original practice emails: 30 question-6 stimuli and 30 question-7 stimuli. These are not ETS past papers. Every stimulus has sender, recipient, subject, message, three explicit response requirements and a 10-minute allowance. Public practice uses D1 without generation or grading calls. Admin: `/quan-ly-he-thong-123?tab=toeic-emails`.

Each of the 50 real-photo groups belongs to a complete parent exam with four explicit references: picture set, email 6, email 7, essay 8. The 30 new email pairs cycle across those 50 parents; existing essay IDs are reused. Editing a title does not change a link. The previous default sets and legacy email records remain supported. Seed markers preserve admin edits and deletions.

The full flow has four screens: five picture sentences → email 6 → email 7 → opinion essay. Practice tabs have independent prompts and drafts. Full submission freezes the linked IDs and all eight answers; retries reuse successful section results.

Native ratings: pictures 0–3 each (15), emails 0–4 each, essay 0–5. Total raw marks: 28. No invented linear conversion to the official 0–200 scaled score. Email/essay scores are a validated integer holistic model estimate, not an average of diagnostic criteria or a word-count formula. Email feedback checks the actual Directions with exact evidence. No automatic 25-word email or 250-word essay ceiling.

Format and rubric reference: [ETS Speaking and Writing examinee handbook](https://www.ets.org/content/dam/ets-org/fr/pdfs/toeic/toeic-speaking-writing-examinee-handbook.pdf).

Verification covers idempotent D1 seeds, 60 distinct emails and positions, stable parent references, rubric bounds and evidence, all eight answers, retry retention, independent practice tabs and responsive admin previews. Browser/provider tests use fixtures; production smoke makes public D1 requests without paid grading or personal submissions.
